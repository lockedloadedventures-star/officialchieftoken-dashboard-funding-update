Current market status

The CHIEF contract is deployed and verifiable on Base, but no market price is being represented here. A price will only be established when CHIEF and WETH liquidity are deposited into the Uniswap pool. Until then, CHIEF should be treated as having no established trading value, and the pool should not be described as actively liquid.

Tokenomics and owner controls

  • The deployed contract mints an initial supply of 1,000,000 CHIEF to the initial owner.
  • The owner can call mint(address,uint256) without a hard-coded maximum. Future issuance is therefore possible and may dilute holders.
  • Token ownership remains held by the published owner EOA above; the company treasury is a separate 2-of-3 Safe and does not change token-owner permissions.
  • There is no claim here that liquidity is locked, that the contract is renounced, or that the token has an audit.
