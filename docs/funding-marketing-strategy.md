# CHIEF Five-Part Funding Marketing Strategy

This plan uses the existing CHIEF website, transparency page, Base dashboard, 2-of-3 company Safe, Stripe donation link, and Utility Pass checkout. It seeks voluntary support and paid service revenue without presenting CHIEF as an investment or promising token value.

## 1. Convert transparent supporters

**Offer:** voluntary one-time card donations through Stripe or crypto donations to the published Base Safe.

**Execution:**

- Make `https://officialchieftoken.com/donate` the only funding call to action.
- Publish one weekly build update showing a completed deliverable and the next funding need.
- State exactly what support may fund: documentation, security review, builder resources, and operating costs.
- Link the Safe, Stripe checkout, privacy policy, and donation terms every time.

**Measure:** page visits, checkout starts, completed donations, average donation, and repeat supporters.

## 2. Sell the Utility Pass as a service

**Offer:** educational briefs, practical templates, builder resources, and eligible office hours through the existing checkout page.

**Execution:**

- Publish one sample brief or template before asking for a subscription.
- Show the deliverables calendar, cancellation/refund terms, and service limitations.
- Keep the service separate from CHIEF ownership, price, liquidity, or investment claims.
- Use customer feedback to improve the next deliverable rather than using price speculation as marketing.

**Measure:** landing-page conversion, trial-to-paid conversion, churn, deliverables published, and support response time.

## 3. Build a proof-based content loop

**Offer:** useful public education that earns attention before a funding request.

**Execution:**

- Publish two short educational posts each week: one Base/contract verification lesson and one liquidity or wallet-safety lesson.
- Use the verified contract, owner disclosure, dashboard, and transparency page as primary sources.
- End each post with one clear next action: read disclosures, use the dashboard, view the Utility Pass, or support the build.
- Correct errors publicly and preserve dated updates.

**Measure:** qualified site visits, saves/replies, transparency-page visits, and attribution to donations or subscriptions.

## 4. Use accountable partnerships and grants

**Offer:** a defined project brief for builder, education, and ecosystem-support programs.

**Execution:**

- Prepare a one-page application with scope, milestones, budget, wallet custody, and evidence links.
- Route company funds through the 2-of-3 Safe and publish received funds and material expenditures where appropriate.
- Ask for non-investment support: infrastructure credits, educational sponsorship, tooling, or a grant.
- Disclose every paid partnership, affiliate relationship, or sponsored post.

**Measure:** qualified applications, introductions, grant decisions, funds received, and milestones delivered.

## 5. Create a responsible community funnel

**Offer:** a public community focused on research, building, and accountability.

**Execution:**

- Run one weekly public office hour or written Q&A.
- Use polls to identify the next useful template, dashboard improvement, or educational topic.
- Welcome viewers without requiring a token purchase or donation.
- Never use fake urgency, fake testimonials, wash trading, undisclosed endorsements, or claims of guaranteed returns.

**Measure:** returning visitors, useful contributions, event attendance, qualified leads, and completed community-requested deliverables.

## 30-day operating cadence

- **Monday:** publish the weekly deliverable and funding allocation target.
- **Tuesday:** publish a short contract, wallet, or liquidity education post.
- **Wednesday:** share a transparent progress update with links to evidence.
- **Thursday:** hold the community Q&A or office hour.
- **Friday:** publish metrics: visits, supporters, service revenue, and completed work.
- **End of month:** publish a plain-language treasury and milestone recap.

## Non-negotiable claims policy

- Describe CHIEF as deployed on Base; do not describe an unpriced token as actively liquid or tradeable.
- Do not promise appreciation, profit, safety, listings, liquidity, audit completion, or investment returns.
- Donations do not provide CHIEF, equity, ownership, profit participation, or investment rights.
- Keep the token-owner EOA separate from the company treasury Safe in every disclosure.
- Use the official website, BaseScan, Stripe checkout, and Safe address as canonical links.

Educational and informational only. Nothing here is financial, legal, or tax advice. CHIEF is speculative and may lose all value.
