Proposed milestones

1. Publish four source-linked explainers

Status: proposed

  • How to verify a Base token contract and read token metadata.
  • How owner permissions, including minting, affect a token.
  • How to assess liquidity and why a pool listing alone does not establish a usable market.
  • Wallet safety, transaction review, and multisig treasury basics.

Each explainer will link to primary sources, include a last-updated date, and disclose its limitations.

2. Improve read-only verification tools

Status: checker foundation published; additional work proposed

  • Document data sources, timestamps, and unavailable checks.
  • Improve mobile accessibility and clear error states.
  • Add repeatable tests for displayed contract and market data.

No tool will sign transactions or replace independent contract/security review.

3. Release reusable builder templates

Status: proposed

  • Contract and project due-diligence checklist.
  • Wallet and transaction review worksheet.
  • Release QA and known-limitations template.
  • Plain-language treasury and milestone update template.

Published templates will be linked from this page and the public repository.

4. Report progress and use of funds

Status: reporting framework proposed

  • Post dated milestone updates with links to completed outputs.
  • If project funding is received, report verified receipts and expenditure categories.
  • Record delayed, changed, or incomplete milestones and explain why.

No grant receipt, spending, partnership, audit, or milestone completion is claimed here without supporting evidence.

Schedule and accountability

The sequence and dates will be finalized only after program eligibility, scope, and available resources are confirmed. A funding application is not an award, and proposed budgets are not commitments or actual expenditures. Material changes will be reflected here with a dated update.

CHIEF is speculative. These deliverables are for public education and software/documentation work; they do not promise token price appreciation, liquidity, investment returns, or a scholarship award.
